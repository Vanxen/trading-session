The 6 Enemies
FOMO

Chasing price after the move. Late entries kill R:R.

Greed

Not taking profit, moving TP, over-sizing after wins.

Fear

Closing winners too early or hesitating on good setups.

Revenge

Re-entering immediately after a loss to “get it back”.

Overconfidence

Increasing size after wins and ignoring the plan.

Hope

Holding losers hoping they will turn around.
